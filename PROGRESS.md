## 2. Invariants & Security Boundaries Verification

1. **Worker Free Access**: Workers never pay recruitment fees (0 FCFA). Companies pay workers directly; Kazi invoices only its own recruitment service fees to the company.
2. **Staff Selection & Client Approval Boundary**: Kazi staff perform selection; company approval is separate from worker confirmation. Public signup grants 0 staff capabilities. Controlled staff provisioning by Super Admin.
3. **Candidate Privacy**: Zero exposure of worker phone numbers, CNI identity documents, or staff internal notes to company clients (strictly enforced in `CandidateCompanyDTO` scoping).
4. **Dual Confirmation Gate (KZ08)**: Company acceptance starts 24h worker availability countdown. Worker confirmation within 24h advances proposal; expired confirmation or terms revision mismatch rejected.
5. **Atomic Assignment Concurrency (KZ09)**: Atomic finalization validates dual confirmation, verifies capacity (`CAPACITY_FULL`), and verifies worker schedule anti-overlap (`WORKER_OVERLAP`). Idempotency supported.
6. **Pointage & Bilan Closure**: Daily pointage revision resets worker confirmation to `PENDING`. Worker dispute requires explanation; staff resolves. Missions cannot be closed while attendance days remain unresolved.
7. **Thread Privacy**: `STAFF_ONLY` internal notes are completely filtered out for worker and company clients.
8. **Preliminary Invoicing**: All preliminary invoices carry a prominent `SAMPLE` watermark and status. External banking receipts enforce unique references; reversals are non-destructive compensating negative entries.
9. **Configuration & Audit**: Unregistered themes or raw user-supplied palettes are rejected. Activations are audited. Staff grant revocation immediately terminates active sessions. Secrets and credentials are automatically redacted in audit logs.
